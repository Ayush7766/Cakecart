About Us

Welcome to Nikita_art, your local destination for artisanal, homemade treats. We specialize in bespoke cake designs and a diverse range of gourmet cupcakes, all tailored to your specific cravings. Whether you need a show-stopping centerpiece for a major event or a box of assorted cupcakes for a small gathering, we bake everything fresh to order with zero shortcuts. Our passion is simple combining professional artistry with the warmth of home-baked goodness. Let us help you celebrate life’s sweetest milestones with a custom creation that is uniquely yours!
